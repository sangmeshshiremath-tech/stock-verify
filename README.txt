STOCK VERIFY - INSTALLABLE APP

Files (all at the top level): index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, icon-maskable-512.png

1. Upload all files to a free web host, e.g. GitHub Pages. Suggested repository name: stock-verify
2. Open the web address on your phone in Chrome. Stay online the first time.
3. Chrome menu (3 dots) > "Install app". Tap Install.
4. Open "Stock Verify" from your home screen. It opens full screen with no address bar.

Notes
- First open must be online: the app saves the Excel reader and fonts on the phone. After that it works offline.
- Data saved in the old Claude page does NOT move to the new app. Finish and download your statements first.
- Reports (Excel, PDF, ZIP) download to your phone Downloads folder.
