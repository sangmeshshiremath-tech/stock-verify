Stock Verify

bySangmesh Hiremath

Upload the branch stock sheet. When someone calls out the last 4 digits of an LR, type them and mark what you see on the floor.

How it works

  1. Upload the stock sheet you get at the branch.
  2. Type the last 4 digits the person calls out. The matching LR opens with its NOA.
  3. Tap All found, the number of articles you have, or Not found.
  4. Open Summary at the end and match it with the printed sheet.

Colour code

  • Green All articles found
  • Orange Short, like 2 of 4
  • Red Nothing found
